# Simple review prompts

Use one of these in the same video chat.

## Interactive review

```text
Review my understanding of this video. Quiz me one question at a time, starting
simple and getting harder. Wait for my answer, explain the exact gap, and update
review.md with my real weak areas at the end.
```

## Review an explanation you already wrote

```text
Here is my closed-book explanation:

<paste your explanation>

Check it against the video notes. Tell me what is correct, what is missing or
wrong, and why. Then ask one follow-up interview question at a time.
```

You do not need to specify a review stage or mode.
